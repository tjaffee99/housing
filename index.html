    About this map

    Every occupied home in the United States and Puerto Rico is on this map: about 85 million owner-occupied homes, colored by what their owners say they are worth, and 45 million rented homes, colored by monthly gross rent (rent plus utilities). Zoomed in, each dot is 10 homes; zoomed out, each dot stands for more (the panel shows how many).

    Hover or tap anywhere for the area's numbers; click for details. From the keyboard, focus the map, move with the arrow keys and press Enter to open the area under the crosshair.

    Where the numbers come from

    Prices are from the US Census Bureau's American Community Survey 5-year estimates for 2020–2024 (tables B25075 home value and B25063 gross rent), in 2024 dollars, for each of the country's 242,000 census block groups. A block group's dots are drawn from its own price distribution, so a dot's color is the price bracket of the homes it stands for, not a neighborhood average. Switch Display options → Area medians to see medians instead.

    Where the dots go

    Dots are placed at random inside the block group's 2020 census blocks, in proportion to how many housing units each block had in the 2020 Census, so they land where homes are and not in parks, lakes or empty land. A dot's exact position is not the location of any real home.

    Things to know

    • Home values are owners' own estimates, not sale prices, and the survey's 5-year window ends in 2024; prices in hot markets have moved since.
    • The Census Bureau caps its brackets at $2 million+ for value and $3,500+ for rent.
    • Block-group estimates come from small samples and have wide margins of error. Tract and county figures are more reliable.
    • Vacant homes (for sale, for rent, seasonal) are not shown. Renter homes with no cash rent are shown in gray.
    • The 2024 release has no figures for 39 block groups in Suffolk County, NY (most of Brentwood and Wyandanch). Their homes are placed using the county total minus the published block groups.
